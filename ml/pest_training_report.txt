INTERPRETATION
--------------
73.5% top-1 on 41 fine-grained pest species with a 1.58 M parameter nano model is in the range the
literature would predict for IP102-style data, and it is the honest number: the split is md5-clean,
pixel-verified against cross-split near-duplicates (242 train and 29 val images quarantined), and the
test split was never used to pick anything. Expect far more than the disease model's 0.95 gate to be
needed here - at 73.5% top-1 many correct answers sit below 0.95, so the pest feature needs its own
confidence-gate sweep (same procedure as ml/confidence_gate_sweep.py) before shipping.

NO FIELD VALIDATION YET: unlike the disease model (16 real field photos), there are no real-world
pest photographs on this machine. Every number above is in-domain IP102 close-ups of insects. Nobody
has yet pointed this model at a farmer's photo, so the app-side framing matters: IP102 images are of
the insect itself, and the dataset has no "no pest present" class.
