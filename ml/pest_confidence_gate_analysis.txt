READ THIS
========================================================================================================
The disease model ships a 0.95 gate because at 0.95 every answer it showed was correct. The
pest model is a harder task (41 fine-grained insect species, a nano backbone), so expect the
opposite trade: a high gate buys precision and costs most of the coverage. Pick the threshold
from the table above against what the app should do when it is not confident - the same
decision the disease model faced in ml/confidence_gate_analysis.txt.

Reminder: whatever threshold is chosen, it has NOT been validated on real farmer photos.
