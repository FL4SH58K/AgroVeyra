3. WHY THE LEAKY-VAL SUBSET FELL 96.67% -> 92.78%
====================================================================================================================
The comparison is sound: both runs scored the SAME 180 files (deterministic sorted()[:3] per class,
filename sets verified identical), same preprocessing, same 60-class list. Only the model differs.

3a. What the rebuild did to those 180 files (ml\dataset_rebuild_manifest.csv, 178/180 matched)
    train,      split_rebuilt                  85   are now training images of the NEW model
    val,        split_rebuilt                  53   still val in the clean split
    quarantine, same_class_duplicate           37   deleted from the dataset (duplicate)
    quarantine, mislabeled_val_wheat_healthy    2   deleted from the dataset (bad label, section 5)
    quarantine, cross_class_duplicate           1   deleted from the dataset (same pixels, other label)

3b. NEW model accuracy by that disposition (measured from the CSV, not inferred)
    train,      split_rebuilt                  85 files   correct 85   wrong  0   (100.0%)
    val,        split_rebuilt                  53 files   correct 52   wrong  1   ( 98.1%)
    quarantine, same_class_duplicate           37 files   correct 27   wrong 10   ( 73.0%)
    quarantine, mislabeled_val_wheat_healthy    2 files   correct  1   wrong  1
    quarantine, cross_class_duplicate           1 file    correct  0   wrong  1
    => every one of the 13 wrong rows is a file the rebuild deleted or an honest val photo;
       ZERO are files whose own path the rebuild moved into the NEW model's training set.

3c. The whole delta sits in one place: the .png (augmented wheat) tail
    extension   files   NEW wrong   NEW acc      OLD wrong   OLD acc
    .jpg          135        1       99.3%          1        99.3%
    .png           45       12       73.3%          5        88.9%
    On the 135 .jpg frames the two models are IDENTICAL (134/135 each). All 12 .png misses are
    wheat classes (aphid, common_root_rot, leaf_blight, mite, stem_fly, tan_spot, healthy), and the
    45 .png frames are exactly 3 per class from the 15 wheat classes.

3d. Why the wheat .png frames got worse - the rebuild cut those classes hardest
    (the wheat set was the duplicated/mislabeled part of the merge; dataset_rebuild_report.txt)
    class                   train before -> after       quarantined   val before -> after
    Wheat___septoria         1,144 -> 279   (-76%)          865          70 ->  70
    Wheat___smut             1,310 -> 420   (-68%)          855          70 -> 105
    Wheat___black_rust         576 -> 264   (-54%)          316          70 ->  66
    Wheat___leaf_blight        842 -> 492   (-42%)          296          70 -> 124
    Wheat___stem_fly           234 -> 137   (-41%)          132          70 ->  35
    Wheat___mite               800 -> 616   (-23%)           99          70 -> 155
    Wheat___tan_spot           770 -> 569   (-26%)          128          70 -> 143
    The frames this sample picks are largely the duplicate-removed ones: the OLD model had many
    redundant copies of them in its leaky train split, the NEW model has 23-76% fewer wheat training
    frames and no longer memorises them.

3e. The decisive caveat - the new model was scored on pixels it had TRAINED on
    6 of the 12 quarantined misses (mite_18, mite_19, tan_spot_10, common_root_rot_119,
    leaf_blight_101, aphid_27) have a BYTE-IDENTICAL copy in merged_clean\train, verified by
    SHA-256: merged_clean\train\Wheat___mite\mite_18.png is a HardLink whose digest
    9B67DD83A808B66968DCBC9700FAE82F8663D26FFD0A9974B41A70180D6C4B55 equals the leaky-val file's.
    The model still misclassifies images that are pixel-identical to its training data. Checks that
    rule out the obvious explanations:
      * PIL vs cv2 decode of those .pngs is IDENTICAL (max pixel difference 0) - not a loader
        mismatch between the training pipeline and the eval script;
      * the images are not degenerate: the 12 wrong .pngs have median std 68.1 vs 50.6 for the 33
        correct ones, and none is near-flat;
      * it is not the resize: the Ultralytics resize+center-crop transform changes the clean-val
        result by only +1.1 pts and the field set not at all (section 4).
    The OLD model trained on the same pixels (the old merged\train held the same hardlinks) and also
    fails them, so the most likely reading is label noise inside the augmented wheat subset - the
    same defect that made the rebuild quarantine 1,000+ wheat files. Treat the .png tail of this
    sample as unreliable ground truth, NOT as a model regression.

BOTTOM LINE for 3: the -3.89 pts is the leak evaporating plus the wheat duplicate tail losing its
memorised copies. On the honest .jpg frames the two models score the same, so this is not evidence
that the leak-free retrain made the model worse.

5. WHAT THE "WHEAT LABEL FIX" ACTUALLY DID
====================================================================================================================
  * it was a QUARANTINE (removal), not a relabel: dataset_rebuild_report.txt records
    "mislabeled : Wheat___healthy" with quarantine reason mislabeled_val_wheat_healthy = 50 files,
    described as "val/Wheat___healthy: source says healthy, the trained model says yellow_rust".
    Those 50 files now sit in ml\data\quarantine and are in NEITHER split.
  * the trigger was the model's own vote, so it is a heuristic and it is self-fulfilling: both the
    OLD and the NEW model label the one sampled example (val\Wheat___healthy\healthy_test_1.png)
    Wheat___yellow_rust at 100.00%. If that picture really is yellow rust, removing it is right; if
    it is a hard healthy-wheat photo, the class lost 50 val images of a rare appearance.
  * the field evidence shows no benefit for healthy wheat: healthy_wheat_1.jpg is still correct but
    its confidence fell 76.18% -> 59.86% (now below the app's 0.70 gate, so it is withheld), and
    healthy_wheat_2.jpg is still wrong (Wheat___aphid 24.7% -> Wheat___leaf_blight 27.6%).
  * that class went from train 1,000 -> 788 and val 70 -> 198, with 84 files quarantined in total.
    Whether the confidence drop comes from the smaller training class or from those extra val
    images is NOT established by this run - it needs the class-wise confusion report
    (ml\class_error_report_clean.txt), not a 16-photo field set. OPEN ITEM.


====================================================================================================================
6. VERDICT - domain gap or dataset?
====================================================================================================================
  The DOMAIN GAP is the dominant limitation, and dataset cleanliness does not touch it:
    * 43.75% (7/16) before, 43.75% (7/16) after; top-3 50.00% both times. Deleting 3,493
      duplicate/mislabeled files, re-splitting so that no photo spans train and val, retraining
      leak-free and re-exporting changed NOTHING about behaviour on real farm photos.
    * the errors are cross-crop / cross-disease confusions the training distribution cannot teach
      away: rice_brown_spot -> Wheat___leaf_blight, tomato_early_blight ->
      Cotton___bacterial_blight 92.3%, wheat_yellow_rust -> brown_rust, rice leaf blight ->
      Rice___Sheath_blight both times. The curated photos are close-up single leaves, evenly lit;
      the field photos are whole-canopy phone shots with mixed backgrounds and framing.
    * the same model scores 91-94% in its own domain, so the network and the export are healthy.
    * neither dataset cleaning nor the app's resize policy moves the field number, and the leaky
      val subset cannot be used to argue otherwise (sections 3 and 4).

  What the step-6 work DID buy: data integrity (0 leaks, 0 in-split duplicate groups, 0 cross-class
  conflicts), a verified 60-class contract, a flatbuffer proven faithful to the checkpoint, and an
  honest baseline to measure against. What it cost: on these 16 photos the app now shows 11 answers
  instead of 8 while only 6 are right (gated precision 87.5% -> 54.5%), and healthy_wheat_1.jpg is
  withheld even though it is correct.

  Recommendations, highest expected payoff first:
    1. grow ml\real_world_test well beyond 16 photos - one photo is 6.25% and every field
       conclusion in this report is one image away from flipping;
    2. treat the field gap as a COLLECTION problem, not a cleaning problem: real phone photos of the
       crops that matter, labelled, added to the training mix (or a second fine-tune stage on them);
    3. re-tune the 0.70 gate against the field set - today it displays 5 wrong answers and hides 1
       right one;
    4. the augmented wheat .png subset is the in-domain weak spot (black_rust 0/3, tan_spot 0/3 on
       the clean val sample; smut 17/20 in the parity run) and it is the subset the rebuild cut by
       23-76%. Fresh real wheat photos are the fix, not more augmentation;
    5. retire ml\data\merged\val as a test set - it is still leaky, and 47% of the sample used here
       is now inside the new model's own training data. Use ml\data\merged_clean\val.

7. CONFIDENCE GATE RE-TUNE - what the app should show now
====================================================================================================================
Sections 1-6 established that the rebuild did not move field accuracy. It did change how much a high
score is worth, so the 0.70 gate - tuned on the model that was replaced in step 6 - had to be
re-measured before shipping. This section is pure post-hoc analysis of the CSVs section 1 already
wrote; no new inference, no GPU, no retrain.

    ml\.venv\Scripts\python.exe ml\confidence_gate_sweep.py   ->  ml\confidence_gate_analysis.txt
    inputs: real_world_results.csv, real_world_results_centercrop.csv,
            val_subset_results_cleanval.csv, val_subset_results.csv

"shown" = the gate lets the answer reach the result screen; below the gate the app asks for a retake.

DECISION: 0.95. It dominates the old 0.70 on this set rather than trading against it:

  * every correct answer 0.70 showed is still shown (6 of them). healthy_wheat_1 (59.86%) was already
    being withheld by 0.70, so raising the gate to 0.95 costs no correct field answer at all;
  * all 5 wrong answers 0.70 let through are now withheld - rice_bacterial_leaf_blight_1 (76.06%),
    rice_bacterial_leaf_blight_2 (89.09%), rice_brown_spot_2 (74.94%), tomato_early_blight_2 (92.30%),
    wheat_yellow_rust_1 (59.73%);
  * the in-domain price is 13 of 180 photos (coverage 91.67% -> 84.44%) and precision goes up
    96.36% -> 98.03%, i.e. the gate does not start rejecting good in-domain answers wholesale;
  * the real price is retake rate: 6 of 16 field photos now get a diagnosis, 10 ask for a retake.

WHY NOT 0.90, WHY NOT HIGHER
  0.90 would show exactly one more answer than 0.95 - tomato_early_blight_2 at 92.30% - and that answer
  is wrong, so it buys nothing but a 20-point precision loss on this set. Nothing above 0.95 is needed:
  no photo in the 16 sits between 0.95 and 1.00 except the correct ones.

THE CAVEAT THAT MATTERS (sweep section 5)
  The 100% is a property of the resize path, not of the model. The same 16 photos scored with
  Ultralytics resize+center-crop show 6 answers and 1 wrong at 0.95 (83.33%), because
  wheat_yellow_rust_2 moves from 70.35% to 99.70% - still wrong - under that transform. A confidently
  wrong answer therefore exists above any usable gate, and in-domain the ceiling is ~98% precision
  (3 of 152 shown val answers are wrong at 0.95). Treat the gate as a UX guard, not a correctness
  guarantee. It is also tuned on 16 photos, where one photo is 6.25%: re-check it as
  ml\real_world_test grows. The backend keeps its env override so the value can be lowered without a
  rebuild.
