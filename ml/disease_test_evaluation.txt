CAVEATS - read before quoting the number
======================================================================================================================
  1. The TEST half shares no source leaf with the SELECTION half, but both come from the val
     split that was monitored during training for early stopping. The model-selection check
     above reports whether best.pt is merely the last epoch: if the weights are identical, no
     epoch was chosen on val and this is a genuine held-out number.
  2. Val images are PlantVillage-style augmented families - several rotations of one leaf on a
     plain background. In-domain accuracy therefore overstates a phone photo of a leaf in a
     field; the field-photo row above and ml/field_failure_analysis.txt are the honest pair.
  3. Everything is scored through the SHIPPED TFLite asset with the app's preprocessing, so it
     is the number the phone produces, not the checkpoint's best-case score.
